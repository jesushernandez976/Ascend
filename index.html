About Ascend Spine & Rehab

Our mission is to give you the tools to rise above the limitations of pain and reclaim a life where you no longer have to avoid the things you love. By uncovering the fundamental barriers holding you back and treating the root cause, we partner with you to deliver individualized care that empowers you to live fully and freely.

Meet Dr. Shane Bruni

"Are you tired of persistent pain or physical limitations that are holding back your performance? If you’ve tried other physical therapy or chiropractic approaches without lasting results, we offer a different path. We focus on the unique demands of athletes like you, whether you’re hitting the box for CrossFit, logging miles on the road, or lifting your baby from the floor, we go beyond simply treating symptoms. By taking a whole person and rehab-focused approach, the underlying imbalances affecting your strength, mobility, and stability are addressed. We will identify the root cause of your issues and create a personalized plan to not only alleviate pain but also elevate your athletic performance and overall health."

We believe whether you’re into CrossFit or an active mother, you should be able to live the life you deserve free from pain killers and the fear of pain ever holding you back.
